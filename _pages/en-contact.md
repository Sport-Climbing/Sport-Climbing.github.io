# Contact

If you have a question about something published on Sport Climbing, or a suggestion, a scientific source or a correction that would improve it, you can write to us by email.

## How to reach us

**Editorial email:**

[sportclimbing@kavehrs.com](mailto:sportclimbing@kavehrs.com)

## What you can write about

- Reporting an error, or sending a correction to something we have published
- Pointing us to books, scientific papers and reliable climbing sources
- Suggesting a subject for an instructional or research article
- Scientific, specialist or editorial collaboration
- Comments and ideas for improving the site

Please say what the message is about. If it concerns a particular page, include the title or the address of that page.
