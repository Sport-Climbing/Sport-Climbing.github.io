This outline describes the kind of journey a Damavand ski project is, so that an inquiry can be specific. It is not an itinerary you can reserve. Contact us for current expedition details and availability.

## What the days are really about

A party flies into Iran, usually through Tehran, moves to the Haraz side of the mountain, and then waits on weather and on how people feel at altitude. The skiing, if it happens, is on the volcano or on a neighbouring objective when the summit is not in condition. The number of nights, the huts or houses used, and the rest days are decisions for a real plan. They are not listed here, because listing them would look like a product.

## What is not included, because it is not yet defined

There is no published price. There is no departure date. There is no named guide, and this site does not claim a certification it cannot show. If a journey is later agreed, the services that are included and the ones that are not will be written down for that journey, not copied from a template.

## How to inquire

Say when you could travel, how many of you there are, what you have skied, and how high you have slept. The [Damavand page](/destinations/damavand/) is the place to read the geographic limits before you write.
