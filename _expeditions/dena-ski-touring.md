This is an outline of ski touring in the Dena, written so an inquiry has something solid to answer. It is not a trip you can join on a date. Contact us for current expedition details and availability.

## What has to be true before a plan exists

The crest has to hold skiable snow. The approach valley has to be open, including any protected-area rule that applies that season. The party has to be content with a long ridge rather than a single famous line. None of those is guaranteed by this page. The [Dena page](/destinations/dena/) records the disagreement about the summit height and the shape of the massif.

## Still unpublished

Price, dates, nights, transport, and who would guide. Those are blank until they are real. The inquiry form is the way to start that conversation.
