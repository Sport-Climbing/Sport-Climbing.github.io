Ski touring on Zard Kuh is a way to travel a long Zagros crest, not a lift-served week. This page does not sell a week. Contact us for current expedition details and availability.

## Shape of a trip, without a timetable

People who ski here usually need a city transfer into Chaharmahal and Bakhtiari, a base around Kuhrang or Chelgerd, and then days chosen from the weather and the snow. Some days are valley approaches. Some are on the crest. A timetable that pretends otherwise would be fiction. The [Zard Kuh page](/destinations/zard-kuh/) explains the geography and the elevation figure we are willing to print.

## Unpublished on purpose

No price, no departure, no included hotels, no guide biography. If those become real for a particular party, they will be stated for that party.
