This page is an orientation for ski mountaineering around Alam Kuh. It is not a departure. Contact us for current expedition details and availability.

## What the journey would have to solve

The massif asks for a base on the Kelardasht side, time to reach the high basins, and a plan that still works if a line under the wall is out of condition. Skiing and short sections of climbing can belong to the same day. They also might not. A serious outline names the objective before anyone talks about a price. This one deliberately does not name a couloir.

## What remains unpublished

Duration, difficulty grade, price, included nights, and the name of any guide. Those facts do not exist as a standing offer. Inventing them would make the page look bookable. It is not.

Read the [Alam Kuh page](/destinations/alam-kuh/) before you inquire, including the note that one of the photographs shows a climbing rope rather than a ski descent.
