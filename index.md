## Iran, for people who travel on skis

Iran is not a ski resort with a different flag. It is a country with two major mountain systems, the Alborz in the north and the Zagros through the west and south, and with winters that put snow on summits above 4,000 metres. Damavand, the volcanic high point of the Alborz, is the mountain most visitors can already name. Alam Kuh, Zard Kuh, and the Dena crest are different kinds of ground: a technical massif, a long Zagros ridge, and another long ridge whose maps do not even agree on the height.

Ski mountaineering here means unsecured terrain, road approaches that close, and days organised around weather rather than a lift clock. The same mountains are also places people hike in summer. A summer path is not a winter ski line, and this site does not pretend that it is.

What you can do here is read the four destination pages, see which photographs are winter and which are not, and send an inquiry if a journey is worth planning. Prices, guides, and dates are not invented to fill the gaps. Where a fact is disputed, the page says so.
